## 💡 Interesting Concepts & Notes
- Used `Gobuster` for the first time and seeing it find hidden directories was an exciting revelation for me. This made me excited now!
- It scans URLs or domains for its hidden or unsecured pages. For example, `gobuster -u http://url.com -w wordlist.txt dir`, the `-u` is the website/url/domain and also we use a `-w` to read a wordlist to take a list of possible page name.

## 🚧 Challenges Faced

## 🔗 Connect the Dots
